Got Questions?

Contact & Onboarding FAQ

Common questions answered for founders, CMOs, and marketing directors.

How quickly will a strategist respond to my submission?

Our average response time during business hours is under 30 minutes. All submissions receive a dedicated audit response within a maximum of 2 business hours.

What is the minimum ad spend required to work with Stackly?

We work best with brands spending $5,000/month or more across paid channels (Google Ads, Meta, LinkedIn, TikTok) to ensure statistical significance for testing.

Do you require long-term lock-in contracts?

No. We operate on performance-driven month-to-month or 90-day growth cycles with clear KPI milestones. You retain 100% ownership of all ad accounts and creative assets.

What happens during our initial 30-minute strategy call?

We review your current acquisition costs (CAC), target ROAS, conversion funnel, and deliver an actionable 3-part growth quick-win tear-down before pitching services.
